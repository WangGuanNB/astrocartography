# Refund Policy – AstroCarto

**Version / last updated:** September 11, 2026

## Scope and when this version applies

This policy covers Standard and Professional one-time purchases and Plus Monthly and Plus Yearly subscriptions, when available. Your order confirmation identifies your plan, the amount paid and its billing period. Listing a subscription here does not mean it is currently offered for sale.

This version applies only to purchases made after it is first published on our website. The version available when you purchased continues to apply to earlier orders, including legacy 2-Week Access Pass purchases and refund requests already submitted. The update date above is not a retroactive effective date. For existing subscriptions, changes to future renewal terms require any notice and consent required by applicable law; this update does not change an already-paid billing period.

The voluntary change-of-mind rules below are separate from remedies for duplicate charges, non-delivery, technical faults or a service materially different from its description. Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer rights.

## 1. Standard and Professional: one-time purchases

We offer a full refund for a change of mind when **both** of these conditions are met:

- You submit your request within 7 days (168 hours) of the successful payment.
- You have used the paid AI service fewer than 3 times under that purchase when you submit the request: **0, 1 or 2 completed uses qualify; 3 or more do not qualify for this voluntary guarantee.**

The same threshold applies to Standard and Professional. Buying a larger credit pack does not increase the number of trial uses. Unused credits alone do not create a right to a change-of-mind refund after these limits. One-time purchases do not automatically renew.

Requests concerning non-delivery, faults, duplicate billing or services not as described are reviewed separately under section 4, even if these time or usage limits have been exceeded.

## 2. How we count use

For this policy, **one completed use** means one user-submitted AI question that receives a complete response and incurs its normal credit charge. Each completed follow-up question counts separately, even within the same conversation. A new chat window or saved conversation is not the unit of measurement.

A separately requested, successfully delivered paid AI report, including a full city comparison report, counts as one completed use, regardless of its credit cost. Viewing an existing answer or report again does not count as another use.

For example, two completed questions in one conversation count as two uses; a third completed follow-up brings the total to three. One completed question and one completed paid report also count as two uses.

We do not count:

- Free map generation, navigation, opening a chat or rereading saved results.
- Free-trial use or use before the relevant payment.
- Failed, empty or interrupted responses that did not deliver the requested output.
- Duplicate requests or charges caused by a technical error.
- Replies that only state that the requested analysis cannot be provided, with no substantive analysis of the requested question.

A success event or a credit deduction alone does not settle a complaint about an unusable or materially defective response. We review the actual issue separately.

We assess usage as of the time you first submit the refund request, not the time we finish reviewing it. Where an account has multiple purchases or credit sources, we review the records to identify usage attributable to the relevant purchase; we do not automatically treat all account history as usage of the latest order. If attribution is unclear, we will review it with you rather than assume you exceeded the limit.

## 3. Plus Monthly and Plus Yearly subscriptions

### First subscription payment

For your first Plus subscription payment, whether monthly or yearly, we offer a full change-of-mind refund if you request it within 7 days (168 hours) of that payment and have fewer than 5 completed paid AI uses under that subscription. The counting rules in section 2 apply: 0–4 uses qualify; 5 or more do not qualify for this voluntary first-payment guarantee.

This first-payment guarantee is not restarted by renewing, switching billing frequency or cancelling and subscribing again. Section 4 and mandatory consumer rights still apply.

### Cancellation and renewals

Cancelling stops future renewal charges; it does not by itself refund an already-paid period. You may cancel using the subscription management option supplied with your account or purchase confirmation. If you cannot access it, contact **support@astrocarto.org** promptly so we can help.

Unless a refund ends the entitlement earlier, cancellation normally leaves access active until the end of the paid period. For a yearly plan, this is the paid year, not the next monthly credit allocation.

If you did not intend to renew, contact us within 7 days of the renewal charge. We will review the request, including use since renewal, cancellation records and any billing problem. This is a review window, not a blanket promise that every renewal is refundable. Outside the voluntary first-payment guarantee, unused subscription time or credits do not automatically qualify for a full or prorated change-of-mind refund.

A charge after an effective cancellation, an incorrect charge or a failure to provide paid access is reviewed under section 4 regardless of these voluntary limits.

## 4. Billing errors, non-delivery and service problems

These cases are **not automatically rejected because of the change-of-mind usage threshold or seven-day window**:

- **Duplicate charges:** We refund a confirmed duplicate charge for the same purchase.
- **Paid credits or access not delivered:** We investigate and restore what was purchased or provide the appropriate refund if delivery cannot be completed.
- **Technical problems or materially defective output:** We review the problem and, where appropriate, first offer a correction, regeneration or other remedy within a reasonable time and without significant inconvenience.
- **Service materially different from its description at purchase:** We review the advertised feature and what was actually delivered. A generated answer alone is not proof that the promised service worked.
- **Incorrect renewal or cancellation billing:** We review the billing and cancellation records and correct confirmed errors.

If an appropriate remedy cannot be provided, we offer a full or partial refund as appropriate to the affected purchase or service, subject to applicable law. You do not have to accept bonus credits, an access extension or a retention offer in place of a refund to which you are entitled. Mandatory rights may permit a refund without first attempting a correction.

Disagreement with an interpretive opinion or the absence of a predicted life outcome is not, by itself, proof of a fault: astrocartography interpretations are informational and entertainment content, not guarantees. This does not excuse inaccurate calculations, missing purchased features or misleading descriptions.

If we discontinue a paid service before completing our obligations, we will provide an appropriate remedy for undelivered credits, access or the unused service period. We do not treat undelivered obligations as fulfilled merely because a plan was labelled one-time or credits had no expiry.

## 5. How to request a review

Email **support@astrocarto.org** or use our website contact/feedback form. Please include:

- The email address used for the purchase.
- Your order number or transaction reference, if available.
- A brief explanation of the issue and the relevant conversation or report, if available.

You may use “Refund Request” as the subject, but a specific subject line is not required. If your account and purchase can already be identified, you do not need to resubmit solely because you omitted an order number. Do not send passwords, full card details or other sensitive payment credentials.

We aim to review requests within **1–2 business days**. If more information or investigation is needed, we will explain what is needed and provide an update. We will tell you the decision and its reason. These service targets do not extend any mandatory legal deadline.

## 6. Refund processing and account effects

Approved refunds are normally initiated within **3–5 business days**, or sooner if required by law, to the original payment method. Your bank or payment provider controls when the funds become visible; we send confirmation once the refund is processed.

A full refund means the amount paid for the refunded purchase; we do not deduct our payment-processing costs from an approved full refund. We cannot control independent currency-conversion differences or fees imposed separately by your bank.

A full refund ends the credits and paid entitlements supplied by that purchase. We will explain any account adjustment and will not remove unrelated purchases or their credits and entitlements. For partial refunds, we explain the refunded amount and the remaining service before processing. We do not require you to accept extra credits instead of an applicable refund.

## 7. Mandatory consumer rights

This voluntary policy does not waive or restrict rights that cannot be excluded under the law applicable to your purchase, including remedies for faulty, undelivered or misdescribed digital content or services.

Statutory withdrawal rights and exceptions differ by jurisdiction and by the type of digital service. Merely accessing the site, using AI or exceeding a voluntary usage threshold does not by itself establish that a statutory right has been waived. Any required express consent, acknowledgment or contract confirmation must be obtained as required by applicable law.

You do not need to cite a law to report a problem. If relevant, we may ask your country of residence to assess which mandatory protections apply. Nothing here prevents you from using a payment provider's applicable dispute process.
